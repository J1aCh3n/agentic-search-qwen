# Employee Handbook

All content in this handbook is synthetic demo data for the Agentic Search project.

## 1. Working Hours and Remote Work

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 16:00 Eastern Time. Outside those hours employees
choose their own schedule as long as they deliver against agreed milestones.

The company is remote-friendly. Engineers may work remotely up to four days per week.
Every team holds one shared on-site day at the Toronto office, and each team picks that
day for itself. Employees who want to work from outside Canada must request approval at
least three weeks in advance, and a single trip may not exceed sixty calendar days
because of tax and insurance rules.

New joiners work from the Toronto office for their first two weeks so that onboarding,
equipment setup, and security training can be completed in person.

## 2. Paid Time Off

Full-time employees receive twenty days of paid vacation per calendar year. Vacation
accrues monthly and up to five unused days may be carried into the following year. Any
carried days expire on March 31.

Sick leave is separate from vacation and is capped at ten paid days per year. A doctor's
note is required only when sick leave exceeds three consecutive working days.

Parental leave provides eighteen weeks at full salary, and it can be taken in at most two
separate blocks within the first year after birth or adoption.

Requests for more than five consecutive vacation days must be submitted in the HR system
at least fourteen days ahead so the team can plan coverage.

## 3. Expense Reimbursement

The daily meal allowance during business travel is 85 Canadian dollars per person. Hotel
spending is capped at 260 Canadian dollars per night in Toronto, Vancouver, and New York,
and at 180 Canadian dollars per night elsewhere.

Every expense above 25 Canadian dollars requires a receipt. Expenses must be submitted
within thirty days of the transaction date; claims submitted after that window need
written approval from a department director.

Any single expense above 1,000 Canadian dollars requires manager approval before the
money is spent, not after. Client entertainment always requires prior approval regardless
of amount.

Reimbursements are paid with the next payroll run if the claim is approved at least five
business days before payday.

## 4. Equipment and Home Office

Each engineer receives a laptop refreshed every three years, plus a one-time home office
budget of 750 Canadian dollars for a desk, chair, or monitor. Receipts for the home office
budget must be submitted within the first ninety days of employment.

Broken or lost equipment must be reported to IT within one business day. Personal devices
may not be used to access production systems.

## 5. Performance and Promotion

Formal performance reviews happen twice a year, in June and December. Promotion decisions
are made only during the December cycle. Employees need at least six months in their
current level before they are eligible for promotion.

Every employee also has a monthly one-on-one with their manager, and a written development
plan is updated at least once per review cycle.
